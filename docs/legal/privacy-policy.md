## 1. Two roles, and why the difference matters

We handle personal data in two distinct capacities, and your rights differ
depending on which applies.

**As a controller** — for data about *you as a user of the Service*: your
account details, how you use the platform, and our correspondence with you. We
decide why and how this is processed, and this policy governs it.

**As a processor** — for data your organisation puts *into* the platform
("Customer Data"): your AI model records, risk registers, incidents, vendor
assessments, uploaded evidence, and any personal data those contain. Your
organisation decides why and how that is processed; we act on its instructions.

If you are an employee or contact of one of our customers and want to know how
your organisation uses Certifyi AI, or wish to exercise rights over Customer
Data, contact your organisation first — it is the controller. We will support it
in responding, and will refer such requests to it.

## 2. What we collect

### 2.1 Information you give us

| Data | When | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | Registration | Identify you in the workspace and in audit records |
| Email address | Registration | Account identity, sign-in, security and service notices |
| Password | Registration | Authentication — stored only as a salted hash, never in plain text |
| Organisation name | Registration | Place you in the right workspace |
| Role / job function | Registration | Set an initial permission level and tailor onboarding |
| Support correspondence | When you contact us | Answer your question and keep a record of it |

Registration collects **name, email, password, organisation and role** — nothing
more. We do not ask for payment card numbers in the application; where a paid
plan applies, billing is handled separately and we do not store full card
details.

### 2.2 Information created by your use of the Service

- **Authentication and session data** — sign-in events, session tokens, and the
  timing of them, to keep your account secure.
- **Audit records.** The platform maintains an audit trail of state-changing
  actions. Each entry records **who acted** (the authenticated user), what
  module and record was affected, the action taken, the time, and — where
  relevant — the values before and after the change. This is a core compliance
  feature, not optional telemetry: it is what makes governance activity
  traceable. It also means your actions in the platform are attributable to you.
- **Technical logs** — IP address, browser and device type, and error
  diagnostics, used for security, abuse prevention and debugging.

### 2.3 What we do not do

- **We do not sell personal data.** We never have.
- **We do not use advertising trackers.** The application contains no Google
  Analytics, Meta, Segment, Mixpanel, PostHog or similar advertising or
  behavioural-analytics tag.
- **We do not use Customer Data to train AI models** — neither our own nor any
  third party's — and we do not use it to build features for other customers.
- **We do not access your workspace content** except where you ask us to for
  support, where it is necessary to fix a fault or address a security issue, or
  where the law requires it. Such access is limited, purposeful and logged.

### 2.4 Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and browser storage that are **strictly necessary** to run the
Service: keeping you signed in, maintaining your session securely, and
remembering interface preferences such as theme and sidebar state. We do not use
advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, so there is no advertising consent
banner to dismiss.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **correct** inaccurate or incomplete data;
- **erase** data, where there is no overriding basis to keep it;
- **restrict** or **object to** processing, including profiling;
- **portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format;
- **withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting prior processing; and
- **complain** to a supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these, email **get@certifyi.ai**. We will respond within
**30 days**, and will tell you if we need longer and why. We may ask you to
verify your identity — we will not use that information for anything else.

Note the interaction between erasure and audit records: an audit trail exists so
that governance activity remains provable. Where we cannot delete an entry
without destroying the integrity of a record your organisation is legally
required to keep, we will explain that, and will restrict processing instead
where appropriate.

## 8. Security

We take security seriously and describe it honestly.

**What we do:**

- **Encryption in transit** — traffic to the Service is protected with TLS.
- **Encryption at rest** — data is encrypted at rest by our infrastructure
  providers.
- **Tenant isolation enforced at the database.** Access is restricted by
  row-level security policies applied in the database itself, and the scoping
  value is set server-side rather than supplied by the browser. A user cannot
  read another organisation's records by manipulating a request.
- **Role-based access control**, so users see what their role permits.
- **Passwords are never stored in plain text**; authentication is handled by our
  managed authentication provider.
- **Audit logging** of state-changing actions with an attributed actor.
- **Least-privilege access** for our own staff, used only as described in §2.3.

**What we do not claim:** Dignep Group does **not** currently hold a SOC 2
Type II, ISO/IEC 27001 or equivalent certification for the Certifyi AI platform.
The platform helps *you* prepare for such audits; that is not the same as us
having completed one. We would rather say so than imply otherwise. If that
changes, this section will change with it.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a personal data breach
affecting you, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by
applicable law, without undue delay.

## 10. Automated decision-making

We do not make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about
you based solely on automated processing.

The Service itself contains automated and AI-assisted features that score,
classify, draft or flag records for *your organisation's* governance work. Those
operate on Customer Data under your organisation's control, and are designed to
support a human decision rather than replace one. Your organisation is
responsible for the human oversight of those features.
